General Information and Projects

Gipuzmeshs Technical and Social Commitment: Technology, Sustainability, and Analysis

Who are we?

Gipuzmesh is a technological project developed in the Microcomputing Systems and Networks Program curriculum. Our goal is to provide autonomous radio networks and Mesh nodes in areas without electrical or telephone infrastructure, ensuring reliable communication.

Gipuzmeshs Sustainability Plan and the SDGs

Environmental management and ecological communication networks plan based on the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):

3 Selected Sustainable Development Goals (GJH)

GJH 9

Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure

Deploying autonomous mesh networks in natural environments and at events, without the need for intrusive cabling or heavy physical infrastructure.

GJH 11

Sustainable Cities and Communities

Ensuring autonomous, ecological, and secure radio communication points in isolated areas and emergency situations.

GJH 12 & 13

Responsible Consumption and Climate Action

Promoting solar energy, LiFePO4 recargable battery , and circular walkie-talkie rental practices.

PSAI Sustainability Goals

  • Solar energy-powered autonomous networks will supply 85% of the power for 2026.
  • 100% reuse or recycling of old batteries and walkie-talkies.
  • Reducing CO2 emissions by 2.4 tons through the use of autonomous mobile networks.
  • 11,950 € budget for 2 years to finance clean ecosystems and solar kits.

Summary of the Action Plan and Budget (Excel)

  • Use of Solar Energy (%85): 85% of the power for mobile networks will be supplied by solar energy in 2026, completely eliminating diesel generators.
  • Equipment Recycling (100%): 100% of old batteries and walkie-talkies will be reused or recycled.
  • Reduction of Emissions: 2.4 tons of CO2 emissions will be reduced through the use of autonomous mobile networks.
  • Budget and Investment: 11,950 € budget for 2 years to finance clean ecosystems and solar kits.
